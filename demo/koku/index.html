Turkish perfume search

Pick one of 183 Turkish evaluation queries. Left: an off-the-shelf multilingual embedder over the raw English perfume data. Right: the final system, a BGE-M3 fine-tuned on teacher-written queries over Turkish perfume text. Descriptions under each perfume were written by the distilled 0.6B student model. Everything was computed offline; this page only replays it.
